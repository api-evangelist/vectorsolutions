# Vector Solutions

> Vector Solutions is a training and workforce management software company serving education, public safety, government, manufacturing, construction, and gaming industries (Vector LMS, Vector EHS, Vector Scheduling, Vector Check It, Acadis, Guardian Tracking, TargetSolutions). Its TargetSolutions online training and records-management platform exposes a REST API (v1) for managing sites, users, profile groups, supervisor and feature access, credentials, courses, and training assignments. Authentication uses a customer-specific AccessToken header over HTTPS; tokens are issued by an account manager.
